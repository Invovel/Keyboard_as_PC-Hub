# GPIO discovery test — 2026-10-16

ESP32-S3 on COM5, ESP-IDF 6.1. Test firmware built, flashed and hash-verified.
Input pull-ups only; no HID, LCD or I2S output. Poll interval 10 ms.

The user performed keys 1 through 4 followed by encoder operation. The
following mapping is inferred from that ordered test, rather than the earlier
wiring descriptions. Times are device uptime in milliseconds.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Key 1 | 42 | 1149772 / 1149932 | Press and release observed |
| Key 2 | 41 | 1151432 / 1151592 | Press and release observed |
| Key 3 | 40 | 1152962 / 1153082 | Press and release observed |
| Key 4 | 38 | 1154862 / 1155022 | Press and release observed |
| Encoder rotation | 1 | Repeated edges from 1156682 to 1160302 | One changing channel observed |
| Final press, presumed encoder switch | 39 | 1161552 / 1161892 | Press and release observed; label inferred from sequence |

No GPIO2 edge appeared during this ordered test. Rotation direction and detent
counts are NOT validated: two phase signals were not captured, and 10 ms polling
can miss short transitions. This does not establish a defective encoder or an
electrical wiring fault. A subsequent direction test should use faster sampling
or interrupt capture before drawing a hardware conclusion.

The four button inputs have passed a single press/release check, not endurance,
simultaneous-input, HID or application integration acceptance. Earlier GPIO0
observations predate the user's changes and are not the current key-1 mapping.

Source of evidence: live COM5 serial monitor, project gpio_smoke. No claim is
made that the earlier manual wiring table matches the final observed pin map.
